The Story

Meet Benny

13Where it really started

I got my first pair of clippers at 12. By 13 I was cutting for real, practicing on myself, my three older brothers, my dad, and every friend who'd sit still long enough.

Through high school I kept sharpening, kept learning, kept blending. When I got into Computer Science at uOttawa I had to slow it down for a minute and focus on the books, but I could never put the clippers down for long.

Born and raised in Ottawa to Iranian and Polish refugees, hard work is kind of the whole story. Now I've found my rhythm, and honestly? I just love this. Every fade, every lineup, every person in the chair.

— Benny
